# Story

## Product

What it is, in one sentence a stranger would understand. The job it does. How it
does it differently. The moment a user first gets it (the "aha"). Quote the
product's own words, with the page they came from.

## Audience

Who watches this, and where (feed, landing page, launch post). What they already
use, believe, and are tired of. What would make them stop scrolling.

## Insight

The one true thing about this audience the video turns on. Not a feature.

## Proposition

The single idea the viewer leaves with.

## Arc

The format (launch, feature, changelog, explainer), the protagonist, the device,
and the acts in the format's shape. For each: what the viewer sees, and what they
understand after it. Name the signature moment and why only this product can
claim it.

## Mode

Animated, captured, or hybrid, and why for this product.

## Look

Accent, palette, type, and brand art found on the site, with sources.

## Voice

The product's own voice in three lines, quoted from its pages, and the call to
action from the brief.
