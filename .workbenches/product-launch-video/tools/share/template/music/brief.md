# Music brief

Written after the arc is fixed. The score is generated outside this run from this file, so
every line must be concrete enough for someone who has not seen the video.

## Length

The video's length in seconds, and the length to generate (the video plus about 4 seconds, so
the track can be cut on a downbeat and faded over the end card).

## Moments

One line per moment the music must hit, with its time from the manifest: where energy enters,
where it builds, the reveal or turn (the drop or the biggest change), any held breath, and the
end card. Say what the music does at each, not what the video shows.

## Style

Tempo in BPM first. Then the energy and attitude in plain words a listener would use
("funky, playful, confident", "tense, then bright and fast"), then the genre and the
instruments that carry that energy, then production. Chosen for this product's voice and this
audience (a terminal tool and a consumer app do not share a score). Never a mood adjective
alone: "warm", "handmade", "friendly", "uplifting", "inspiring", "corporate" came back as hold
music every time. Instrumental.

## Avoid

Two or three things this score must not be, named exactly. Never vocals.
